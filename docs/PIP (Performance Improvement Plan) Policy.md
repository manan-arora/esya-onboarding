# 1 **Purpose** 

The Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) is a structured framework designed to support employees who are not meeting expected performance standards. It aims to provide clear feedback, define measurable improvement goals, and offer guidance and support to help employees succeed in their roles. The intention of a PIP is developmental, not punitive, while ensuring organisational performance standards are upheld.

# **Scope** 

This policy applies to all confirmed, full-time employees of the organisation identified as consistently underperforming relative to their role expectations, KRAs, behavioural standards, or PMS ratings. Employees on probation or serving their notice period are excluded from the PIP process.

# **PIP Duration** 



PIP duration is determined based on severity: 

|**Duration**|**Cases**|**Review Frequency**|
|---|---|---|
|1 Months|Critical performance gap (Default)|Weekly|
|2 Months|Developmental improvement required (can<br>be placed upon prior approval)|Bi Weekly|



Note: PIP duration can be extended by another month based on the outcomes of the reviews to reach a conclusion. 



# **PIP Process** 

- 5.1 Initiation 

   - The Reporting Manager, in coordination with HR, assesses performance gaps and determines the PIP type, duration, and objectives/goals. 

   - A formal discussion is held with the employee to communicate concerns. 

   - A written PIP and counselling document is issued, including: 

      - Specific concerns and measurable objectives 

      - Improvement timeline 

      - Expectations & KRAs 

      - Support/resources available (training, coaching, etc.)

   - Employees are required to acknowledge receipt of the PIP document, and any dispute 

      - or clarification must be raised before such acknowledgement. 

- 5.2 Monitoring & Check-Ins 

   - Regular review meetings held as per PIP type (Refer Point 4) 

   - Progress observations and scores documented 

   - Employee receives continuous feedback from their reporting manager 

- 5.3 Performance Scoring 

   - Each review is scored on a three-point rating scale, which is Good, Satisfactory and Unsatisfactory 

   - If the employee scores unsatisfactory in consecutive reviews: 

         - Employee may be advised to resign; failing which, termination may be processed 

## 5.4 Final Review 

Based on the employee’s performance at the end of the PIP: 

|**Outcome**|**Action**|
|---|---|
|Successful|Return to the normal performance cycle and may be eligible for salary|
|Completion|revision and any appraisal.|
|Partial Progress|A short period of extension may be granted with HR approval in<br>special cases, such as unavoidable leave of employee or manager|
|Unsuccessful<br>Completion|Separation from the organisation in line with company policy.|





# **Zoho Performance Tracking** 

All PIP documentation must be updated on Zoho People: 

- Manager initiates PIP record and updates all review scores & comments 

- HR monitors and validates entries to ensure fairness & compliance 

- Employee has visibility to PIP scores, feedback, and schedule 

# **Confidentiality** 



All PIP-related discussions and documents will be kept confidential and shared only with the employee, reporting manager, HR, and authorised leadership.
