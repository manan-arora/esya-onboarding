## 3.2 Earned Leave (EL)

Earned Leave is intended for personal purposes such as vacations and
important life events.

### Entitlement and crediting

-   Employees become eligible after completing one full month of
    service.
-   After the first full month, **1.5 days of EL** are credited.
-   Thereafter, **1.5 days of EL accrue monthly**.
-   Employees joining between the **1st and 15th** of the month receive
    **1.5 days** for that month.
-   Employees joining on or after the **16th** receive **0.5 day** for
    that month.

### Carry-forward and balance

-   Up to **12 days** of EL may be carried forward to the following
    year.
-   Total EL balance must not exceed **45 days** at the end of a
    calendar year.
-   Any accumulated EL above 45 days at year-end automatically lapses.

### Planned EL

-   EL of **5 or more consecutive days** must be planned, communicated,
    and approved at least **15 days in advance**.

### During probation

-   EL can accrue and be used during probation.

### During notice period

-   EL is not encouraged during the notice period.
-   If EL is taken during notice period, the **Last Working Day (LWD)
    will be extended** according to the number of EL days taken.

### EL encashment

EL is encashed only on separation or retirement, subject to the policy
conditions:

-   Maximum encashment: **45 days**
-   Calculation basis: **Basic Pay at the time of separation**
-   Employee must have completed at least **240 days of active work**
-   Employee must be a **confirmed employee**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3.3 Sick Leave (SL)

Sick Leave is provided for health-related issues and medical
emergencies.

### Crediting

-   SL accrues at **0.5 day per month** from the date of joining.
-   The policy also specifies **6 days credited at the beginning of the
    year on a prorated basis based on Date of Joining (DOJ)**.

### Use

-   SL is available from the date of joining.
-   SL can be used during probation.
-   SL is not accumulable or encashable.
-   Unused SL lapses at the end of the year.

### Medical documentation

For prolonged sick leave beyond the allotted entitlement, valid medical
documentation is required and will be reviewed by the internal medical
team.

If the required medical proof is not provided, the leave may be treated
as **LOP**.

### During notice period

-   Only SL may be used during notice period.
-   Special approval from the reporting manager is required.
-   Valid medical proof is required.
-   For unplanned absence, inform the manager promptly.
-   SL should be applied on the day of the requirement.
-   For planned medical procedures, notification must be provided at
    least **3 days in advance**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3.4 Casual Leave (CL)

Casual Leave is intended for short-term personal requirements.

-   Annual entitlement: **6 days**
-   Accrues at **0.5 day per month** from the date of joining.
-   Cannot be availed continuously for more than **2 days**.
-   Prior approval from the reporting manager is required.
-   The reporting manager must be informed when CL is being availed.
-   CL can be used during probation.
-   CL cannot be used during notice period.
-   CL cannot be carried forward.
-   CL cannot be encashed.
-   Unused CL lapses at the end of the calendar year.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3.5 Compensatory Off (CO)

Compensatory Off may be granted when an employee is approved to work on
weekends or company-declared holidays.

### Credit

-   **8 hours or more:** 1 full day CO
-   **4 hours or more:** 1 half-day CO

### Important rules

-   CO must be used within **90 days** from the date it was earned.
-   CO cannot be encashed.
-   Reporting manager approval is mandatory for availing CO.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3.6 Maternity Leave (ML)

Eligible regular female employees who have completed **80 days of
service** with the company may receive paid maternity leave of up to
**26 weeks**.

Key provisions:

-   Leave can commence up to **6 weeks before the expected date of
    delivery**.
-   Applies to up to **two children**.
-   In case of miscarriage or medical termination of pregnancy, **6
    weeks of paid leave** is granted immediately following the incident.
-   Adoptive and commissioning mothers are entitled to **12 weeks of
    paid maternity leave**.
-   Nursing breaks are provided upon returning to work.
-   The request must be submitted in advance and approved by the
    reporting manager.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3.7 Paternity Leave (PL)

Eligible male employees who have completed **80 days** with the company
are entitled to **7 days** of paternity leave for the birth of their
children, up to two children.

-   The leave can be availed within **5 days immediately after the
    birth**.
-   Adoptive and commissioning fathers are eligible for **5 days**
    immediately after the process.
-   Requests should be submitted in advance for managerial approval.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3.8 Loss of Pay (LOP)

LOP is unpaid leave and may be planned or unplanned.

Employees must communicate LOP details to the reporting manager and HR
during the leave period.

The policy states that:

-   Unapproved or unauthorized leave may be treated as LOP.
-   Unapproved or unauthorized WFH may be treated as LOP.
-   Employees must resume duty according to the approved leave schedule.
-   Any change to the return plan must be promptly communicated to the
    reporting manager and HR.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3.10 Sabbatical Leave

Sabbatical Leave may be granted for extended academic or professional
development.

Key conditions:

-   Minimum **180 days of continuous service** is required.
-   Sabbatical leave is **unpaid**.
-   Prior approval from Management and HR is required.
-   Sufficient justification and supporting documents may be required.
-   Employees remain covered under Insurance during Sabbatical Leave.
-   Employees taking Sabbatical Leave must commit to returning and
    serving the organisation according to company guidelines.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3.11 Menstrual Leave

The policy provides **12 days of paid menstrual leave per year** for
eligible regular female employees in Pan India.

-   Calculated as **1 day per month**
-   The monthly allotment resets in the next month.
-   It is non-accumulative.
-   It cannot be encashed.
-   The month's allotment must be used within that month.
-   It cannot be carried into the following month.
-   It cannot be clubbed with other leaves except Sick Leave.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

> it will be considered Loss of Pay (LOP).

Sick Leave during notice period requires the special approval and
medical proof described above.

## Card title

**Business Travel**

### Short description

Know when you can travel, how to book it, what is reimbursable, and what
needs prior approval.

### Policy metadata

-   **Policy:** Domestic Travel Policy
-   **Version:** 1.0
-   **Policy date:** 11-Nov-2025
-   **System:** Zoho Expense
-   **Applies to:** Esyasoft employees including Regular, Trainee,
    Intern and FTC employees who are in Zoho HRMS

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4.1 Before you travel

### The basic flow

``` text
Plan travel
   ↓
Raise Trip Request in Zoho Expense
   ↓
Reporting Manager approval
   ↓
Travel Desk / booking process
   ↓
Travel
   ↓
Collect bills and supporting documents
   ↓
Submit reimbursement in Zoho Expense
```

### Important

-   All travel-related bookings must be initiated through a **trip
    request in Zoho Expense**.
-   No travel bookings will be made without a trip request in Zoho
    Expense.
-   Travel should be undertaken in the most cost-effective manner while
    following the policy.
-   Approval must be obtained in advance from the reporting manager.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4.2 Air travel

Air travel is permitted when the travel time between origin and
destination is **more than 6 hours by road or rail**, measured from
boarding point to drop point.

Rules:

-   Flight tickets must be booked through Zoho Expense.
-   Travel Desk bookings are made through an approved travel agency.
-   All employees travel by **Economy Class**.
-   Business / First Class is not allowed unless paid by the client and
    approved by the reporting manager.
-   Frequent flyer benefits may be used as long as they do not increase
    the ticket cost.
-   Business-required flight change fees and in-flight meals may be
    reimbursed.
-   Seat upgrades and excess baggage are not reimbursed unless approved
    by management.
-   Claims must not exceed actual cost and must be supported by
    receipts/invoices where applicable.
-   Airline cancellation or rescheduling must be handled through the
    Travel Desk.
-   Lounge access is not reimbursed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4.3 Road and rail travel

For intercity travel:

-   Rail or bus travel is allowed where travel time is **less than 6
    hours by road or rail**.
-   Travel Desk may book air tickets if the flight is cheaper than the
    train/bus option.
-   Employees may book rail/bus tickets themselves and claim
    reimbursement or use the Travel Desk.
-   If using a personal car, toll and fuel expenses are reimbursed at
    the applicable kilometre rate.
-   Vehicle wear-and-tear or other ancillary expenses are not
    reimbursed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4.4 Intracity transportation

Use the most effective and economical ground transport.

The policy encourages:

-   Public transport such as Metro and airport shuttle/bus services
    where feasible.
-   Autorickshaws where feasible.
-   E-bike services such as Rapido and Yulu where feasible.
-   App-based cab services such as Ola, Uber, Meru or similar providers
    instead of travel agencies.

### Receipts

-   Cab receipts should be submitted for reimbursement.
-   For autorickshaw travel where an invoice is unavailable, a payment
    screenshot can be submitted.

### Personal vehicles

Employees may use personal vehicles where it is more economical.

Current policy rates:

-   **Car:** ₹15/km
-   **Two-wheeler:** ₹10/km

The applicable distance is from employee residence/office to the client
office or meeting location and back.

These rates are subject to annual review.

> **Exception:** The policy specifically notes that project requirements
> such as Alfanar may have separately approved fuel reimbursement
> amounts configured in Zoho.

### Other rules

-   Toll, bridge, highway, ferry and parking charges are reimbursable
    where applicable.
-   Employees travelling to the same location are advised to share rides
    where possible.
-   Employees whose regular/daily workplace is a client or Discom office
    are not eligible for intracity transportation under this provision.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4.5 Hotel / accommodation

Hotels/accommodation are provided for business trips requiring an
overnight stay.

Process:

-   Raise the hotel request through Zoho Expense.
-   Travel Desk books from approved hotels based on cost-effectiveness
    and convenience.
-   If a preferred hotel is unavailable, the Travel Desk considers
    quality, hygiene, convenience and cost-effectiveness.
-   For a changed schedule:
    -   To extend the stay, inform the Travel Desk at least **1 day
        before the original checkout date**.
    -   To check out earlier, inform the Travel Desk at least **1 day
        before the actual checkout date**.
-   Check out on the day of travel before the hotel's checkout time.
-   Cloakroom charges may be reimbursed at actuals where necessary.
-   Personal damages/mishandling of hotel property are the employee's
    responsibility.
-   Hotel claims are subject to the policy limits and supporting bills.
-   Hotel rooms booked directly by the Travel Desk are handled according
    to the Travel Desk booking arrangement.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4.6 Guest houses

Where an Esyasoft guest house is available and feasible, employees
should use it instead of a hotel.

Rules:

-   Maximum normal stay: **2 weeks**
-   If stay exceeds 2 weeks, necessary Management/HR approval is
    required every week.
-   If stay exceeds 3 months, HRA may be deducted based on the city.
-   Exceptional HR & Management approval may allow the deduction to be
    written off.
-   Damage/mishandling of guesthouse property may be recovered from the
    employee.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4.7 Meals / food

-   Employees initially pay for their meals.
-   Reimbursement is made at actuals with supporting receipts, subject
    to applicable limits.
-   Travel advances may be obtained from Finance before travel.
-   Receipts are required for meal purchases when claiming actual meal
    expenses.
-   Alcohol is not reimbursable unless it is related to a client
    business meeting and the required proof of the client meeting is
    submitted.
-   Grade-wise limits are provided in the policy annexure.

### Per diem

Per diem is an alternative policy allowance for food and other expenses
during a business trip.

-   Bills are not required for the per diem claim.
-   A trip report must be submitted and approved in Zoho Expense by
    Management and Finance.
-   Entitlement depends on actual travel duration.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4.8 Business meetings / client entertainment

Employees who need to entertain clients for business purposes may claim
eligible expenses subject to prior approval.

Requirements:

-   Obtain manager / Department Head approval by email before submitting
    the bills.
-   Claims must have supporting receipts/bills.
-   Client entertainment can include breakfast, lunch and dinner,
    subject to the policy and client requirements.

The policy identifies the following roles as permitted to incur client
entertainment / client business / employer branding / hiring meeting
expenses:

-   Senior Management
-   Sales VP / Directors / Managers
-   Delivery VP / Managers
-   HR Head / delegate
-   CFO / Finance Managers

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4.9 Miscellaneous expenses

Laundry may be reimbursed on actuals with receipts when the business
trip is longer than **4 days**.

Examples of non-reimbursable expenses:

-   Personal care items
-   Clothing or clothing rentals
-   Personal entertainment
-   In-room or in-flight movies
-   Theatre/movie tickets

Employees with approved travel plans may approach Finance for a domestic

Travel advance is intended for other expenses such as:

-   Local conveyance

## 4.12 Booking process

### Raise the request at least 4 working days in advance where possible

The policy recommends raising local travel and hotel booking requests at
least **4 working days in advance**.

Include:

-   Project name
-   Departure date
-   Suitable travel time
-   Mode of transport
-   Departure location
-   Arrival location
-   Return ticket details
-   Hotel location
-   Check-in date
-   Check-out date

The request goes to the Travel Desk after Reporting Manager approval in
Zoho Expense.

Booking confirmations are sent to the employee's email.

Any deviation from the trip schedule must be approved by the Reporting
Manager and updated in Zoho.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4.13 Reimbursement process

After returning:

1.  Open Zoho Expense.
2.  Raise the Expense Reimbursement Form.
3.  Enter:
    -   Date
    -   Amount
    -   Category
    -   Location
    -   Business purpose
    -   Project name
4.  Attach original receipts.
5.  Submit the reimbursement report.
6.  Obtain Reporting Manager approval.

### Deadline

The reimbursement report must be submitted within **30 working days of
returning from the trip**.

If a travel advance was provided, the money utilization report must be
submitted within **15 days of reaching the office** for domestic travel.

Late submission without reasonable cause may result in disciplinary
action, including penalties where applicable.

## 5.5 Salary revisions and promotions

The policy states:

-   Appraisal cycle: **April--March**

# 6. PERFORMANCE IMPROVEMENT PLAN (PIP)

## Card title

**Performance Improvement Plan**

### Short description

A structured framework for employees who are consistently not meeting
expected performance standards.

### Policy metadata

-   **Policy:** PIP Policy / Guidelines on Performance Improvement
-   **Version:** 1.0
-   **Revision date:** 04-Feb-2026
-   **System:** Zoho People

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6.1 Purpose

The PIP is intended to:

-   Provide clear feedback.
-   Define measurable improvement goals.
-   Provide guidance and support.
-   Help employees succeed in their roles.
-   Maintain organisational performance standards.

The policy describes the intention of a PIP as **developmental, not
punitive**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6.2 Scope

PIP applies to confirmed, full-time employees who are identified as
consistently underperforming relative to:

-   Role expectations
-   KRAs
-   Behavioural standards
-   PMS ratings

Employees on probation or serving notice period are excluded from the
PIP process.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6.3 Possible PIP triggers

A PIP may be initiated based on:

### Performance / deliverables

-   Failure to meet agreed KPIs, KRAs, targets or deadlines over
    multiple review cycles.
-   Declining productivity or output quality affecting team/project
    goals.
-   Frequent errors requiring rework or excessive supervision.

### Skills / capability gaps

-   Inability to perform core responsibilities at the required
    proficiency level.
-   Lack of improvement after coaching, training or feedback.
-   Difficulty adapting to new processes, tools or role requirements.

### Behaviour / workplace conduct

Only where the behaviour directly impacts performance:

-   Poor collaboration or communication affecting team
    dynamics/outcomes.
-   Unprofessional behaviour or inability to accept responsibility for
    performance gaps.
-   Repeated policy violations despite guidance and counselling.

### Attendance / work discipline

-   Excessive unplanned leave affecting performance.
-   Chronic late attendance or early departure affecting deliverables.
-   Misuse of WFH flexibility affecting productivity, where applicable.

### Customer / stakeholder feedback

-   Complaints or escalations from internal/external stakeholders.
-   Failure to resolve recurring issues despite feedback.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6.4 PIP duration

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Duration                Typical case            Review frequency
  ----------------------- ----------------------- -----------------------
  1 month                 Critical performance    Weekly
                          gap --- default         

  2 months                Developmental           Bi-weekly
                          improvement required,   
                          with prior approval     
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

The PIP may be extended by another month based on review outcomes to
reach a conclusion.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6.5 PIP process

### Step 1 --- Initiation

The Reporting Manager, in coordination with HR:

-   Assesses the performance gap.
-   Determines the PIP type and duration.
-   Defines objectives/goals.
-   Holds a formal discussion with the employee.
-   Issues a written PIP and counselling document.

The written PIP includes:

-   Specific concerns
-   Measurable objectives
-   Improvement timeline
-   Expectations and KRAs
-   Available support/resources such as training and coaching

The employee must acknowledge receipt of the PIP document. Any dispute
or clarification should be raised before acknowledgement.

### Step 2 --- Monitoring and check-ins

-   Regular review meetings take place according to the PIP type.
-   Progress observations and scores are documented.
-   The employee receives continuous feedback from the Reporting
    Manager.

### Step 3 --- Performance scoring

Each review uses a three-point scale:

-   **Good**
-   **Satisfactory**
-   **Unsatisfactory**

If an employee receives **Unsatisfactory in consecutive reviews**, the
policy states that the employee may be advised to resign; if they do
not, termination may be processed.

### Step 4 --- Final review

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Outcome                             Policy action
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  Successful completion               Return to normal performance cycle
                                      and may be eligible for salary
                                      revision/appraisal

  Partial progress                    Short extension may be granted with
                                      HR approval in special cases such
                                      as unavoidable leave of employee or
                                      manager

  Unsuccessful completion             Separation from the organisation in
                                      line with company policy
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6.6 Zoho People tracking

All PIP documentation must be updated on Zoho People.

-   Manager initiates the PIP record and updates review scores/comments.
-   HR monitors and validates entries for fairness/compliance.
-   Employee has visibility of PIP scores, feedback and schedule.

### Confidentiality

PIP discussions and documents are confidential and are shared only with:

-   Employee
-   Reporting Manager
-   HR
-   Authorised Leadership

# 10. POSH --- PREVENTION OF SEXUAL HARASSMENT

## Card title

**POSH & Workplace Safety**

### Short description

Know what behaviour is covered, how to raise a complaint, and how the
Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) handles it.

### Policy metadata

-   **Policy:** Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) Policy
-   **Version:** 3.0
-   **Latest date shown in supplied document:** 10-Aug-2026
-   **Framework:** Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention,
    Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013 and applicable rules

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 10.1 Esyasoft's position

The policy states that Esyasoft does not tolerate verbal or physical
conduct that creates an intimidating, offensive or hostile environment.

Sexual harassment is prohibited and the policy describes a mechanism for
prevention and redressal of complaints.

The policy covers protection and redressal relating to women and men.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 10.2 Workplace includes digital spaces

The policy expressly covers professional digital/online spaces,
including examples such as:

-   Microsoft Teams
-   WhatsApp
-   Social media used for professional interactions
-   Video conferencing
-   Other digital communication platforms

Workplace therefore includes physical premises as well as
virtual/digital spaces where employees interact in the course of
employment.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 10.3 What can constitute sexual harassment?

The policy lists unwelcome sexually determined behaviour including:

-   Physical contact and advances
-   Demand/request for sexual favours
-   Sexually coloured remarks
-   Remarks of a sexual nature about clothing/body
-   Pornography
-   Sexual jokes/teasing/pranks
-   Sexually demeaning or offensive images/material
-   Such conduct through email, SMS, MMS, WhatsApp, Teams, social media,
    video conferencing or other digital correspondence
-   Repeated unwanted requests to socialise or continued expressions of
    sexual interest
-   Sexually suggestive gifts/objects
-   Eve teasing, innuendoes and taunts
-   Physical confinement against one's will
-   Persistent watching, following or contacting
-   Other unwelcome physical, verbal or non-verbal conduct of a sexual
    nature

The policy also identifies circumstances such as:

-   Implied/explicit promise of preferential employment treatment
-   Implied/explicit threat of detrimental employment treatment
-   Threat about present/future employment status
-   Interference with work or creation of an
    intimidating/offensive/hostile environment
-   Humiliating treatment likely to affect health or safety

The policy states that assessment does not depend solely on the person's
intention; the experience of the aggrieved person is relevant.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 10.4 Who is covered?

The policy defines an aggrieved woman/man as an individual of any age,
whether employed or not, who alleges sexual harassment by the
respondent. It includes contractual and temporary workers, visitors and
other covered persons.

The policy's definition of employee also includes people such as:

-   Regular employees
-   Temporary/ad-hoc employees
-   Contract workers
-   Probationers
-   Trainees
-   Apprentices
-   Other covered persons working at the workplace

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 10.5 Internal Complaints Committee (ICC)

The policy provides for an Internal Complaints Committee.

The Committee includes:

-   A Presiding Officer who is a senior woman employee
-   At least two employee members committed to the cause of women
-   One external member familiar with sexual-harassment issues and/or
    legal knowledge

At least half of the total Committee members must be women.

The Committee is responsible for:

-   Receiving complaints
-   Initiating and conducting inquiries
-   Submitting findings/recommendations
-   Coordinating implementation of appropriate action
-   Maintaining confidentiality
-   Submitting required reports

The Committee may receive complaints through email, company intranet or
secure online forms. Virtual hearings may be used where physical
presence is not feasible.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 10.6 How to lodge a complaint

A complaint should include:

-   Details of the incident
-   Available documentary evidence
-   Names of witnesses, where applicable

The policy states that a complaint should be lodged within **3 months**
from the date of the incident / last incident.

The Committee may extend this by another **3 months** where reasons
preventing timely filing are recorded in writing and accepted by the
Committee.

Where a complaint cannot be made in writing, the Presiding Officer or a
Committee member can provide reasonable assistance in putting it in
writing.

The policy also describes circumstances in which another person may
lodge a complaint on behalf of an aggrieved woman with her written
consent.

### Digital evidence

For incidents involving digital communications, the policy states that
screenshots, chat logs and emails can be submitted as supporting
evidence.

Preserve digital evidence carefully and maintain metadata where
possible.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 10.7 What happens after a complaint?

### Initial handling

The person receiving the complaint should:

-   Listen to the complainant.
-   Take the concern seriously.
-   Refer it to the appropriate Committee.
-   Avoid prejudging the situation.
-   Record notes accurately.
-   Maintain confidentiality.
-   Take steps to prevent victimisation or disadvantage.

The respondent and directly involved persons may need to know the
complainant's identity as part of the formal process.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 10.8 Conciliation

Before formal inquiry, the Committee may attempt conciliation **only if
requested by the aggrieved woman/man**.

Key points:

-   Conciliation is a practical resolution mechanism.
-   It does not itself mean the respondent accepts the complaint.
-   If settlement is reached, the Committee records and reports it to
    the employer.
-   Copies of the settlement are provided to complainant and respondent.
-   Once the agreed action is implemented, no further inquiry is
    conducted through that route.
-   The policy states that conciliation should occur within **2 weeks**
    of receipt of the complaint.

Virtual conciliation may be conducted where both parties agree and
confidentiality can be maintained.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 10.9 Formal inquiry

A formal inquiry may proceed when:

-   Conciliation is not requested.
-   Conciliation does not result in settlement.
-   A settlement condition is not complied with and the complainant
    informs the Committee.

The Committee proceeds with inquiry within **1 week** of the relevant
trigger.

During the inquiry:

-   The complainant submits the complaint, supporting documents and
    witness names.
-   A copy is sent to the respondent within **7 working days**.
-   The respondent has **10 working days** to reply with supporting
    documents.
-   Legal practitioners cannot represent parties during the inquiry.
-   The inquiry follows principles of natural justice.
-   At least **3 Committee members including the Presiding Officer**
    must be present.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 10.10 Interim relief

During the inquiry, on written request from the complainant, the
Committee may recommend interim measures such as:

-   Transfer of complainant or respondent
-   Leave for the aggrieved woman for up to **3 months** in addition to
    other leave entitlement
-   Preventing the respondent from assessing the complainant's work
    performance
-   Other appropriate relief

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 10.11 Inquiry timeline and report

The policy states that:

-   Inquiry proceedings are documented.
-   Both parties receive an opportunity to present their side and
    evidence.
-   Witnesses may be interviewed.
-   Cross-examination/questions may be facilitated through the
    Committee.
-   The inquiry, including submission of the Inquiry Report, is
    completed within **90 days from the date the inquiry commences**.

After inquiry, the Committee submits its report with
findings/recommendations to the employer within **10 days of completing
the inquiry**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 10.12 Possible outcomes

If a complaint is substantiated, the Committee may recommend action such
as:

-   Counselling
-   Censure/reprimand
-   Apology
-   Written warning
-   Withholding promotion/increments
-   Suspension
-   Termination
-   Other action considered appropriate by Management

The policy states that Management acts on the recommendations within
**60 days** and confirms implementation to the Committee.

If the complaint is unsubstantiated, the Committee recommends that no
action be taken and communicates closure appropriately.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 10.13 Malicious complaints

The policy states that action may be recommended where the Committee
establishes that a complaint was malicious, knowingly false, or
supported by forged/misleading documents.

Important qualification from the policy:

> Mere inability to substantiate a complaint does not by itself mean the
> complaint was malicious.

The policy also states that inability to provide digital proof does not
imply that a complaint is false or malicious; credibility and context
must be evaluated.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 10.14 Confidentiality

The policy treats the following as confidential:

-   Identity of complainant
-   Identity of respondent
-   Identity of witnesses
-   Statements/evidence
-   Committee recommendations
-   Employer action

Details of complaints or inquiry proceedings must not be shared publicly
or through WhatsApp, Teams or social media.

Breaches of confidentiality may lead to disciplinary action.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 10.15 Appeal

A party dissatisfied with the implementation/non-implementation of
recommendations may appeal to the appellate authority in accordance with
the Act and Rules.

The policy states an appeal period of **90 days from communication of
the recommendations**.

Appeals may be submitted electronically through the designated
email/digital form.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Committee members.

# 15. WEBSITE UX RULES FOR THESE POLICY PAGES

## Do

-   Make the policy pages useful to someone who has never seen the
    policy before.
-   Lead with the practical question: **"What do I do?"**
-   Show important numbers and deadlines prominently.
-   Use tables where they improve scanning.
-   Keep official terminology intact.
-   Clearly show which system is used: Zoho People or Zoho Expense or
    Zoho Recruit.
-   Include source/version information.
-   Provide an obvious path to the official policy.
-   Use expandable sections for detailed rules.
-   Make the first screen useful without requiring the employee to read
    the entire policy.

## Do not

-   Invent HR email addresses.
-   Invent approval chains.
-   Invent policy exceptions.
-   Invent reimbursement amounts.
-   Invent holiday dates.
-   Invent leave eligibility.
-   Turn policy summaries into contractual promises.
-   Hide important conditions in tiny text.
-   Replace the official policy with a paraphrase.
-   Add "common practice" rules that are not present in the supplied
    documents.

# 19. FINAL CONTENT PRINCIPLE

The policy section should feel like an employee asking:

> **"I need to get something done. What do I do?"**

and the website answering:

> **"Here is the process, here are the important rules, here is where
> you do it, and here is the official policy if you need the full
> detail."**

The goal is **not** to reproduce nine PDFs inside a website.

The goal is to turn the supplied policies into a reliable, searchable,
practical employee companion while preserving the authority of the
original documents.

**Official policy always wins.**
