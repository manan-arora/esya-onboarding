Training and Certification Reimbursement Policy 





# **Purpose** 

The purpose of this policy is to provide clear guidelines for employees seeking reimbursement for certification and training expenses. It ensures a standardized process for approval, reimbursement, and accountability, promoting skill enhancement and career growth within the organization. 



# **Scope** 

This policy applies to all employees of Esyasoft (Permanent and Direct Contractors as part of Joint Ventures) seeking reimbursement for certification and training costs from external vendors. It covers the approval process, reimbursement procedure, service agreement requirements, and post-training obligations. 

# **Eligibility** 



Employees are eligible to apply for reimbursement under the following conditions: 

1. The training  and/or certification (voluntary and organization mandated) must be **directly relevant** to the employee’s current role or future career path within the Esyasoft Group. 

2. **Prior approval** must be obtained from the **Reporting Manager, Business Unit Head** and the **Head – Talent Development.** 

3. A **business justification** , outlining the relevance and potential **return on investment (ROI)** , must be provided as part of the request. 

4. Must not be on active notice period. 

5. Must be a confirmed employee. 

# **Approval Process** 



1. **Request Submission:** The employee must submit a formal request via email with course details and business justification to their manager and human resource business partner for review and approval. 

2. **Forwarding Approvals:** Once approved, the employee should forward the approval email, along with the justification note, to the Talent Development team for recordkeeping and further coordination. 

# **Reimbursement Procedure** 



1. **Completion Requirements:** After successfully completing the training or certification, the employee must obtain the **invoice/fee receipt** and the **completion certificate** .

# **Post-Training Obligations** 



Employees are expected to demonstrate a reasonable standard of competence and performance in tasks related to their training or certification. Wherever applicable, employees are encouraged to share the knowledge and skills acquired with their peers to foster a culture of continuous learning and collective growth. 

# **Non-Reimbursement Conditions** 



Employees who breach the service agreement will be liable to repay the training costs in accordance with their tenure as per the agreement. If an employee fails to qualify for the certification on the first attempt, they must bear the cost of any subsequent attempts. Reimbursement is not applicable for exam retake fees.
