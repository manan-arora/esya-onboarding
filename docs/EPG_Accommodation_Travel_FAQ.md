## 2. Is an iron available in the room?

Yes. Each accommodation unit is equipped with an iron and ironing stand.

## 3. What items are NOT provided?

The following items are not provided:

- Toiletries
- Towels
- Garbage Bags/Covers
- Personal Hygiene Products
- Mosquito Repellent
- Personal Medications/Medicines

## 4. Do I need to bring my own toiletries?

Yes. Employees must bring their own toiletries and personal care items.

## 5. Are towels provided?

No. Towels are not provided.

## 6. Should I bring personal medicines?

Yes. Please carry prescribed medicines and medical essentials.

## 7. Is drinking water available?

Yes. Drinking water is available through the Aquaguard water purifier.

## 8. Are cooking facilities available?

Yes. Induction Cooktop, Microwave/Oven, Refrigerator, Kettle and Toaster are available along with basic kitchen utensils.

## 9. Is there a Gym or any other recreational facility available on campus?

Yes. Gymnasium is on 5th floor of capability centre residential area along with recreational facilities such as Table Tennis, foosball and library facilities are available on the campus.

## 9. What should I bring or arrange for a comfortable stay?

- Toiletries
- Towels
- Personal Hygiene Products
- Mosquito Repellent
- Umbrella
- Personal Medicines
- Garbage Bags/Covers (if required)
- Other personal essentials such as tissue paper as needed
