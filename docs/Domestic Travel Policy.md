## Introduction 



The term “Business Travel” refers to any temporary business activity required by the job undertaken by the Employee on behalf of the Employer outside of the residence of the first workplace (stationary working facility). 

The Purpose of this Business Travel Policy is to optimize the overall process and ensure the traveler’s safety and security. It will allow better business travel productivity and ensure that the travel expenses of the company are controlled. Establishing certain standards and laying down some ground rules will help ensure that employees’ trips are safe and cost-effective 

### 1.1 Scope 

The policy includes all the employees of the company. All employees must follow the policy. Following the policy helps keep you safe, helps our company save money, and ensures a streamlined process. Once you understand how the policy works, it’s easy to follow. 

### 1.2 Coverage 

This Policy applies to the following categories undertaking business travel.

## Travel Policies 



### 2.1 Domestic Travel Policies 

- 2.1.1 Air Travel 

   - Air travel is permitted only if the travel time between the origin and the destination is greater than 6 hours by road or rail (from boarding point to drop point).

   - Flight tickets must always be booked through Zoho Expense. Ticket bookings by the Travel Desk are made through an approved travel agency, as they provide savings in domestic travel. 

   - All employees shall travel by Economy Class. Business and First-Class travel is not allowed, unless paid for by the client and approved by the reporting manager. 

   - Employees may use frequent flyer benefits, but the use of these programs cannot increase the cost of tickets. Employees who are enrolled in airline incentive programs are permitted to utilize their personal upgrades on their own, along with the Company’s booking of the lowest available fare. 

   - Any additional expenses due to a business requirement incurred after booking, such as flight change fees and in-flight meals, shall be reimbursed. Any expenses towards upgrading seats & Excess Baggage shall not be reimbursed unless approved by the management. 

   - The travel expenses claimed by the employee should not exceed either the actual cost or must be supported by a receipt/invoice. 

   - Approval must be obtained in advance from the reporting manager before undertaking the travel in Zoho Expense. 

   - For the employees who travel often, the frequent flyer points accrued by the employee can be used for personal purposes or for upgrading the seats when on business travel. 

   - Any cancellation or rescheduling of airline tickets must be done through the Travel Desk only. 

   - Any lounge access by employees will not be reimbursed by the company. 

- 2.1.2 Road / Rail Travel 

   - If the travel time between the destination and the destination is less than 6 hours by rail or road for any intercity travel, only then rail or bus travel is allowed. However, Travel Desk may book air tickets where flight tickets are cheaper than train/bus tickets. In case employees opt to travel by their own car, toll and fuel expenses will be reimbursed at the applicable rate per kilometer. No additional vehicle-related wear and tear / other ancillary expenses will be reimbursed. 

   - Employees may book rail/bus tickets on their own and claim reimbursement or may approach the Travel Desk to book rail/bus tickets. 

- 2.1.3 Intracity Local Transportation 

   - Intracity local transportation applies to all employees who travel for business purposes to other cities. It also applies to employees who regularly work outside of Esyasoft offices and must travel to client offices in the same city for business purposes/business meetings. 

   - Employees must use the most effective and economical means of ground transportation. 

   - Employees are encouraged to use public transport such as Metro, Airport shuttle/bus services, where it is feasible. 

   - Employees should use Autorickshaws, where it is feasible for Intracity travel. The actual amount spent on such travel will be reimbursed. (If the invoice is not available, then a payment screenshot needs to be submitted) 

   - Where feasible, Employees may also use e-bikes, for example, Rapido and Yulu. The actual amount spent on such travel will be reimbursed. 

   - Employees must make use of cab services such as Ola, Uber, Meru or any app-based cab provider instead of booking cars from travel companies/agencies. Receipts must be submitted for such travel for reimbursement. 

   - In case cars are required to be booked from a travel company/agency, for the entire day or half a day, prior approval from the reporting manager is required.

   - Employees may use their personal cars if it is more economical than other means of transportation. The amount spent by the employee will be reimbursed at the applicable rate per kilometer. 

   - Employees will be reimbursed at Rs 15 / km for cars and Rs 10 / km for two-wheelers. (Distance from employee’s residence/office to client office or meeting location and back). The rate/km will be reviewed annually and revised if necessary.

   - If two or more employees are travelling to the same location, they are advised to share the ride wherever possible. 

- 2.1.4 Hotels / Accommodation 

   - Hotels/accommodation will be provided for employees for business trips that requires an overnight stay. 

   - All Employees must raise the request through Zoho Expense for booking hotels/accommodation. Travel Desk will book hotels/accommodation from the list of approved hotels based on cost-effectiveness and convenience, based on the request. If the company's preferred Hotel is not available or does not exist in the city of travel, the Travel Desk will book hotels after considering the quality, hygiene, convenience and cost effectiveness. 

   - If there is a change in the travel schedule, employees must inform the Travel Desk to either extend the stay (Travel Desk must be informed at least one day before the original check-out date) or to check out sooner than the scheduled date (Travel Desk must be informed at least one day before the actual check-out date). 

   - Employees must check out on the day of travel before the hotel’s checkout time. Employees may use the cloakroom facilities provided by the hotel where necessary. Any charges for such a cloakroom will be reimbursed at actuals. 

   - Reimbursement of any expenses incurred by employees will be based on actuals and with supporting bills. If the bills exceed the above limits, the differential amounts will not be reimbursed. However, this does not apply to hotel rooms booked by Travel Desk. 

   - Any damages/mishandling to hotel property must be paid by the employee. 

   - The company reserves the right to verify the bills by checking with the service providers. 

   - The grade-wise upper limits for hotel/accommodation per night is defined in Annexure A, section 4.1 

- 2.1.5 Guest House 

   - In cities where Esyasoft guest houses are available, employees must make use of them (assuming they are available) instead of staying at hotels. Travel Desk will check if the guest house is available and feasible before booking hotel rooms. 

   - Any damages/mishandling to guesthouse property will be recovered from the employee. 

   - The maximum possible stay for an employee would be two weeks, and if the stay is more than two weeks, then necessary management/HR approval should be provided every week.

   - If the stay exceeds more than three months, then HRA will be deducted from the salary based on the city. Only with exceptional approval from HR & Management the deduction may be written off. 

- 2.1.6 Meals / Food Expenses 

   - The Employee should initially pay for his/her own meals, and these expenses will be reimbursed at actuals, duly supported by receipts. Employees may obtain travel advances from the Finance Department before they travel, as per section 2.3 of this document. 

   - Receipts must be attached for all meal purchases while on business for reimbursement. Reimbursement for the consumption of alcohol is not allowed, unless it is related to client business meetings. Proof of such client meetings should be submitted.

- 2.1.7 Expenses for business meetings/ entertainment 

   - Employees who are required to entertain clients for business purposes (such as business discussions, relationship building), while on business travel or otherwise, can seek reimbursement of such expenses. Approval from the manager / Department Head (by email) indicates that the budgeted amount for such expenses must be obtained before submitting such bills. Such claims must be supported with the requisite receipts/bills. 

   - Client entertainment includes the following (assuming such entertainment is permitted by the client organisation): 

      - Breakfast, lunch, dinner 

   - Esyasoft personnel who are allowed to incur client entertainment/client business/employer branding/hiring meeting expenses include Senior Management, Sales VP / Directors / Managers, Delivery VP / Managers, HR Head/delegate and CFO / Finance Managers. 

- 2.1.8 Miscellaneous Expenses 

   - The company will reimburse laundry expenses while on a business trip if the travel duration is greater than 4 days, on actuals supported by receipts. 

   - Items such as the following are not reimbursable: 

      - Personal Care Items 

      - Clothing or clothing rentals 

      - Personal entertainment, like in-room or in-flight movies, theatre or movie tickets. 

### 2.2 Handling Exigencies during Business Travel 

While on travel, employees may face certain demands that may require them to return to their base location immediately. In case of such circumstances, employees should immediately get in touch with the Travel Desk. Necessary assistance will be provided by the Travel Desk. 

### 2.3 Travel Advance for domestic travel 

Employees with travel plans approved by their managers should approach the Finance team for a travel advance for domestic travel through Zoho Expense only. 

Travel desk will book air/train/bus tickets as well as hotel rooms/accommodation for domestic business travel. Travel advance is necessary for other expenses such as local conveyance and food.

## Travel Process 



### 3.1 Travel Booking Process 

- Raise a request for business travel through a trip request in Zoho Expense from the following <u>link.</u> 

- All Local travel and hotel booking requests are recommended to be raised at least 4 working days in advance. 

- Trip request with travel booking details like Project Name, departure date, suitable time for travel, mode of transport, departure place, arrival place, and return ticket details. If any hotel booking is required, please mention the place, the Check-in and Check-out dates. 

- Trip request will go to the Travel Desk after being approved by the respective Reporting Manager in Zoho Expense. Based on the documents and details, tickets will be booked. 

- Travel Desk will book hotels from the list of approved hotels based on cost-effectiveness and convenience. If the company's preferred Hotel is not available or does not exist in the city of travel, the Travel Desk will book hotels after considering the quality, hygiene, convenience and cost effectiveness 

- Once the ticket or accommodation is booked, you will get all confirmations through your Email ID. 

- Any deviation from the trip schedule must be approved by the Reporting Manager, and the same needs to be updated in Zoho. 

- Before check-out, ensure all the bills and necessary documents are collected from the hotel. 

- In case the business trip is for more than 1 month, the expense report must be submitted monthly. 

### 3.2 Reimbursement process 

- The employee should raise the Zoho Expense Reimbursement Form from the Zoho portal and needs to fill the Expenses Reimbursement Form with the date, amount, category, location and business purpose of your claim and Project Name – and always attach original receipts for all your expenses. 

- Within 30 working days of returning from the trip, employees would be required to submit their Expense Reimbursement report in Zoho and get it approved by the Reporting Manager in Zoho. 

- After all the processes and approvals, the accounts department will be crediting your travel expenses amount to your bank account. 

- The employees should submit the money utilization report within 15 days of reaching the office (in case of domestic travel), beyond which disciplinary action will be taken, including penalties for late submission if done without reasonable cause, if advance has been made.
