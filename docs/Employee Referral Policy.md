## Objective 



At Esyasoft, we are committed to hiring exceptionally talented individuals who embody our values and contribute meaningfully to our organizational goals. We believe our employees are in the best position to recommend potential candidates who align with our culture, skills, and vision. To recognize and reward this effort, we offer referral incentives for eligible positions. 



## Scope 

This policy applies to all full-time employees of Esyasoft across all locations in India, except those specifically excluded under Section 4. The policy is valid only for roles officially posted on the Employee Referral Portal via Zoho Recruit. 

## Eligible Positions & Referral Bonus Details 



### 3.1 Eligibility 

Referrals will be considered only if the job position is posted on the Employee Referral Portal. Submissions via any other channel will not be eligible for referral rewards. 

### 3.2 Referral Bonus 

A bonus of ₹20,000 (subject to applicable taxes) will be awarded for each successful hire referred to an eligible position. 

### 3.3 Payout Timeline 

The referral bonus will be disbursed 90 days after the candidate’s Date of Joining (DOJ), subject to the following conditions: 

- Successful completion of the onboarding/orientation process 

- Successful completion of background verification (BGV) 

### 3.4 Referring Employee Eligibility 

To qualify for the bonus, the referring employee must be actively employed with Esyasoft at the time of payout and should not be on notice period.

## Exclusions – Who Is Not Eligible to Refer 



The following categories of employees are not eligible for referral bonuses: 

- Leadership team members 

- Interviewers or hiring managers for the referred position 

- HR/Recruitment team members 

- Rehired employees (if rejoining within 1 year of exit) 

- Referrals that fail background verification (BGV) – If a bonus has already been paid, it will be recovered from the employee 

## Instructions to Refer (via Zoho Recruit) 



All referrals must be submitted via Zoho Recruit to ensure proper tracking and eligibility. 

Steps to refer a candidate: 

1. Log in to Zoho Recruit using your Esyasoft employee credentials 

   - Link: Referrals - Esyasoft Technologies Private Limited Recruit 

2. Navigate to the “Employee Referral” section. 

3. Select the appropriate job opening. 

4. Upload the candidate’s resume and complete the required fields. 

Referrals submitted outside Zoho Recruit will not be considered for the referral bonus. 

## Policy Review & Updates 



This policy will be reviewed periodically by the HR team and may be updated based on: 

- Business requirements 

- Hiring trends 

- Management discretion 

All updates will be communicated and made available on the internal HR portal (Zoho People). Employees are 

expected to stay informed about the latest version of this policy.
