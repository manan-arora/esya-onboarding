# 1 **Purpose** 

The Performance Management System (PMS) is designed to evaluate employee performance fairly and transparently, align individual goals with organisational objectives, and facilitate career growth. This policy ensures a structured and systematic approach to performance evaluation, feedback, and development. 

# 2 **Scope** 

This policy applies to all full-time employees ( _including those in probation_ ) of Esyasoft, except for those who are serving their notice period.

# **Salary Revisions and Promotions** 

|**Appraisal Cycle**|April to March (Annually)|
|---|---|
|**Eligibility Criteria**|Employees who joined on or before the 30<sup>th</sup>of September|



- Employees with an “Outstanding” “Exceeds Expectation” and “Meets Expectation” rating are eligible for increments and career progression. 

   - Employees with a “Below Expectation” are not eligible for increments & career progression. 

   - Employees with a “Needs Improvement” are not eligible for increments & career progression and will undergo a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) as per PIP policy. 

- Employees who joined between 1st October and 31st March will not be eligible for salary revision in the current appraisal cycle. The revision will be considered in the next appraisal cycle based on the cumulative performance rating. 

- Salary revision will be effective from the April payroll. 

# **Calibration** 



The HR and Leadership team will conduct a calibration process to ensure fairness and consistency across departments.
