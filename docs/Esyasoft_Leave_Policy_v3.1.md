# 1. Purpose

Leave is a benefit extended by Esyasoft to promote a healthy work-life balance for employees. It enables them to take time off for rest, recovery, vacations, personal matters, and social commitments. All types of leave must be duly authorised or approved by the respective reporting manager.

# 2. Scope

This Leave Policy applies to all on-roll employees under Esyasoft India.

# 3. Leave Policy

The company offers various types of leave to support employee needs across different life and work scenarios. The types of leave, their eligibility, and the number of days available per calendar year are as follows:

| No. | Leave Type | Paid/Unpaid | Maximum Leave Available Per Calendar Year | Eligibility |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | Earned Leave (EL) | Paid | 18 Days | All regular employees |
| 3.2 | Sick Leave (SL) | Paid | 6 Days | All regular employees |
| 3.3 | Casual Leave (CL) | Paid | 6 Days | All regular employees |
| 3.4 | Compensatory Off (CO) | Paid | Based on work done on rest days/overtime | Employees working on weekends (Saturday & Sunday) or company-declared holidays. Minimum 4 & more hours for half day and 8 & more hours for full day |
| 3.5 | Maternity Leave (ML) | Paid | 26 weeks (including weekends/holidays) | All regular female employees |
| 3.6 | Paternity Leave (PL) | Paid | 1 week (including weekends/holidays) | All regular male employees |
| 3.7 | Leave on Loss of Pay (LOP) | Unpaid | Based on need | All regular employees |
| 3.8 | Company Declared Holiday | Paid | As per Esyasoft Holiday Framework | All regular employees |
| 3.9 | Sabbatical Leave | Unpaid | 6 Months (including weekends/holidays) | All regular employees |
| 3.10 | Menstrual Leave | Paid | 12 Days | All regular female employees (Pan India) |

## 3.1 Earned Leave (EL)

Earned Leave (EL) is provided to employees for personal purposes such as vacations or important life events. Employees become eligible for EL after completing one full month of service, following which 1.5 days of EL are credited. Thereafter, 1.5 days of EL will accrue on a monthly basis.

- Employees who join the company between the 1st and 15th of the month will be credited with 1.5 days of EL for that month.
- Those who join on or after the 16th will be credited with 0.5 day of EL for that month.

### 3.1.1 EL Accumulation and Usage

- A maximum of 12 days of Earned Leave (EL) may be carried forward to the subsequent year.
- The total EL balance shall not exceed 45 days at the end of any calendar year.
- All EL availed for 5 or more consecutive days must be planned, communicated, and approved at least 15 days in advance.

### 3.1.2 During Probation and Notice Period

- Employees are permitted to accrue and utilise EL during their probation period.
- During the notice period, if an employee avails Earned Leave, the Last Working Day (LWD) will be extended accordingly based on the number of ELs taken.

### 3.1.3 Encashment

- ELs will be encashed upon separation or retirement only, subject to a maximum limit of 45 days. (Basic Pay of the salary at the time of separation)
- No ELs are encouraged during notice period.
- To be eligible for EL encashment, the employee must have completed at least 240 days of active work with the organisation and must be a confirmed employee.

### 3.1.4 Rules

- Earned Leave (EL) accrued during a calendar year may be carried forward to the subsequent year, subject to a maximum limit of 12 days for that particular year.
- Any accumulated EL balance in excess of 45 days at the end of the year shall automatically lapse and will not be eligible for carry forward.

## 3.2 Sick Leave (SL)

Sick Leave is granted to employees for health-related issues or medical emergencies. SL accrues at the rate of 0.5 days per month, starting from the employee’s date of joining. SL is applicable from the date of joining and can be availed during the probation period.

SL is not accumulable or encashable, and any unused balance lapses at the end of the year.

For any prolonged sick leave taken beyond the allotted entitlement, employees are required to provide valid medical documentation (which will be reviewed by our internal medical team).

Failure to provide the required medical proof will result in the Sick Leave being treated as LOP.

During the notice period, only SL may be used and only with special approval from the reporting manager and upon submission of valid medical proof (which will be reviewed by our internal medical team).

Employees are expected to inform the manager promptly in the case of unplanned absence. Application of SL should be done on the day of the requirement.

For planned medical procedures, notification must be given at least three days in advance.

## 3.3 Casual Leave (CL)

Casual Leave (CL) is provided to meet short-term personal requirements and cannot be availed continuously for more than two (2) days.

CL accrues at the rate of 0.5 days per month, starting from the employee’s date of joining.

CL cannot be availed during the notice period. It is not eligible for carry forward or encashment. Any unutilized CL balance at the end of the calendar year will lapse automatically.

Employees are required to inform their reporting manager while availing CL, and prior approval must be obtained before proceeding on leave.

## 3.4 Compensatory Off (CO)

Employees who are approved to work on weekends or any company-declared holidays may be granted Compensatory Off.

- A full day's CO is awarded for 8 hours & more of work.
- A half-day CO is awarded for 4 hours & more.
- CO must be utilised within 90 days from the date it was earned.
- It cannot be encashed.
- Approval from the reporting manager is mandatory for availing of CO.

## 3.5 Maternity Leave (ML)

Female employees who have completed 80 days of service with the company are eligible for paid maternity leave of a maximum of 26 weeks.

- This leave can commence up to 6 weeks before the expected date of delivery and applies to up to two children.
- In the case of miscarriage or medical termination of pregnancy, 6 weeks of leave with pay is granted immediately following the incident.
- Adoptive and commissioning mothers are entitled to 12 weeks of paid maternity leave.
- Upon returning to work, nursing breaks will be provided.
- The maternity leave request must be submitted in advance and approved by the reporting manager.

## 3.6 Paternity Leave (PL)

Male employees who have completed 80 days with the company are eligible for 7 days of paternity leave for the birth of their children (up to two children).

- The leave can be availed within five days immediately after the birth.
- Adoptive and commissioning fathers are eligible for 5 days of paternity leave immediately after the process.
- Requests must be submitted in advance for managerial approval.

## 3.7 Leave on Loss of Pay (LOP)

LOP (Loss of Pay) applies to all regular employees and may be planned or unplanned.

- Details of the LOP must be communicated to the reporting manager and HR during the leave period.
- Any unapproved or unauthorized leave or Work From Home (WFH) will be treated as LOP.
- Employees are required to resume duty as per the approved leave schedule.
- In case of any change in the return plan, the employee must promptly inform the reporting manager and HR.

## 3.8 Company Declared Holidays

Company-declared holidays will be observed as per the Esyasoft Holiday Framework and will include:

- National holidays
- State holidays
- Mandatory holidays
- Optional holidays

## 3.9 Sabbatical Leave

Sabbatical Leave may be granted to employees for extended academic or professional development purposes.

- Eligible employees must have completed a minimum of 180 days of continuous service with the organisation.
- Sabbatical Leave is unpaid.
- It requires prior approval from Management and HR, with sufficient justification and supporting documents (if applicable).
- During Sabbatical Leave, employees are covered under Insurance.
- Employees availing Sabbatical Leave must commit to returning and serving the organisation, as per company guidelines.

## 3.10 Menstrual Leave

All women employees are entitled to 12 days of paid menstrual leave per year, calculated as one day per month, which will be reset in the next month.

- Menstrual leave shall be treated as paid leave, and wages shall be provided at the normal daily earnings rate.
- The leave is non-accumulative and cannot be encashed.
- The leave allotment for a given month must be availed within the same month and cannot be carried forward to the succeeding month.
- Menstrual Leave can't be clubbed with any other leaves except Sick Leave.

# 4. Leave Crediting and Eligibility

Leave crediting follows a structured approach to ensure clarity and consistency for all employees. The table below outlines the monthly leave crediting and corresponding eligibility status:

| Leave Type | Monthly Crediting | Availability Based on Employment Stage |
|---|---|---|
| Earned Leave (EL) | 1.5 days credited after completing one calendar month | Credited and can be availed during the probation. EL is not encouraged during notice period. |
| Sick Leave (SL) | 6 days will be credited at the beginning of the year and on a prorated basis based on the Date of Joining (DOJ) | Credited and can be availed during the probation and notice period (with proof and special approval) |
| Casual Leave (CL) | 6 days will be credited at the beginning of the year and on a prorated basis based on the Date of Joining (DOJ) | Credited and can be availed during probation. Cannot be utilised during notice period. |

## 4.1 Leaves During Notice Period

During the notice period, if an employee avails any type of leave mentioned above, except for Sick Leaves, the Last Working Day (LWD) will be extended accordingly based on the number of leaves taken, or it will be considered a Loss of Pay (LOP).

# 5. Process

Employees must apply for all types of leave through the HRMS platform.

It is the employee’s responsibility to obtain the approval.

In the case of unplanned leave, approval may be obtained post-resuming work.
